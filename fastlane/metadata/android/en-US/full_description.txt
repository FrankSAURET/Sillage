<p>Sillage is an offline, local music player that can play .mp3, .flac, .ogg, .opus, .m4a, .mka and .wav files.<br><br>- Endless shuffle: all your songs in random order, reshuffled on every loop<br>- Choose one or more root folders, subfolders included<br>- Android Auto support<br>- Material 3 design with dynamic colours<br>- Browse by album, artist, genre, folder and file system<br>- Playlist management<br>- Synced lyrics (LRC, TTML, SRT), including word-by-word<br>- Full ReplayGain 2.0 support<br>- Works with system and third-party equalizers<br><br>Sillage has no internet access: no data ever leaves your phone. It only plays files stored on the device and cannot bypass copy protection.<br><br>Found a problem? Open an issue: <a href="https://github.com/FrankSAURET/Sillage/issues">https://github.com/FrankSAURET/Sillage/issues</a><br><br>Note: not every audio format works on every device. See <a href="https://developer.android.com/media/platform/supported-formats#audio-formats">https://developer.android.com/media/platform/supported-formats#audio-formats</a><br><br>Sillage is free software under GPLv3 or later, based on Gramophone (<a href="https://github.com/FoedusProgramme/Gramophone">https://github.com/FoedusProgramme/Gramophone</a>). Thanks to its authors, contributors and translators.</p>
